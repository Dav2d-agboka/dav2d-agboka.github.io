Our Story

A Culinary Journey

Redefining African Fine Dining

Since 2014, Graka Eatery has been at the forefront of elevating authentic African cuisine to a premium dining experience. Nestled on the historic Ho-Denu Road in the heart of Ho, Volta Region, our restaurant celebrates the rich culinary heritage of Ghana while embracing modern presentation and technique.

Every dish tells a story of tradition, passion, and the finest locally sourced ingredients. From our signature smoky jollof rice to hand-pounded fufu with palm nut soup, we honor the recipes passed down through generations.
